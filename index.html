Stop the data center.

The University of Michigan plans to construct a $1.2B data center for classified nuclear weapons research and AI computing in Ypsilanti Township. The project threatens residents' health, doubles the university's total carbon emissions, and leaves the Huron River at risk of chemical pollution.

This is environmental racism in action. UM selected a diverse, working-class community that lacks the resources to fight a multi-billion-dollar institution. They have ignored outcry from residents and instead used its special privileges as a public university to bypass local zoning requirements, site reviews, and township votes. When Ypsilanti Township passed a water hookup moratorium on hyperscale facilities to protect its community, the university threatened legal action, claiming the policy was "unlawfully discriminatory" against data centers. Worse, they refuse to sign any Community Benefit Agreements to protect local residents.

We are student organizers mobilizing on campus to hold UM accountable. We refuse to let our university exploit neighboring communities and poison the environment.
